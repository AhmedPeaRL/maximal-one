# Solar Periodicity Nuisance Freeze

## Status

This protocol is a post-observation methodological repair.

It is not preregistered evidence and does not retroactively convert
historical exploratory results into confirmatory evidence.

Claim promotion remains blocked until this nuisance protocol and the
declared stochastic-null protocol are prospectively validated.

---

## Scientific question

The primary sunspot dataset contains known approximately periodic
solar structure. The canonical spectral band may contain harmonics
of the declared reference solar period.

Therefore a spectral separation from a short-memory stochastic null
must not automatically be interpreted as evidence for a non-solar
persistence mechanism.

---

## Fixed reference

Reference solar period:

`132 months`.

Reference fundamental frequency:

`1 / 132 cycles per month`.

Harmonics are evaluated at integer multiples of the fixed reference
frequency.

The harmonic frequencies are not selected from the observed spectral
peaks.

---

## Diagnostic rule

The repository must first report whether any declared solar harmonic
falls inside the canonical frequency band `0.01-0.05 cycles per month`.

This audit is `diagnostic-only`.

It cannot support, reject, or falsify the HCM hypothesis by itself.

---

## No post-hoc band optimization

The canonical band must not be moved, narrowed, widened, or otherwise
optimized after inspecting the nuisance result.

Any future change of the canonical band requires a separately declared
protocol revision and fresh validation.

---

## Nuisance-aware validation requirement

Before scientific claim promotion, the persistence result must be
shown to remain distinguishable under an explicitly declared
solar-periodicity-aware null or residualization protocol.

The nuisance-aware protocol must be fixed before its confirmatory
result is inspected.

---

## Prohibited interpretations

The following are not permitted:

- treating solar harmonic power as independent replication;
- treating a harmonic peak as evidence for HCM;
- changing the frequency band after observing harmonic contamination;
- selecting a null model because it produces a desired p-value;
- converting this diagnostic into confirmatory evidence;
- inferring mechanism or causation from spectral separation alone.

---

## Scientific role

This protocol is a nuisance-control requirement.

It does not establish:

- HCM causation;
- universality;
- consciousness;
- mechanism;
- predictive superiority;
- market advantage.

---

## Promotion rule

No scientific claim may be promoted unless:

1. the stochastic-null protocol is prospectively frozen and validated;
2. the solar-periodicity nuisance protocol is prospectively frozen and
   validated for the primary sunspot domain;
3. the independent real-domain replication requirement is satisfied;
4. clean-checkout computational reproducibility is satisfied;
5. all existing epistemic gates remain satisfied.

Historical exploratory results remain non-confirmatory.
