# Independent Replication Registry V1

## Status

`PRE-EVALUATION REGISTRY`

This registry defines candidate independent real domains before
confirmatory endpoint evaluation.

It does not establish replication.

---

## Required replication

The scientific claim requires at least:

- one primary real domain;
- two independent secondary real domains.

Scientific replication requires domain-level rejection of the same
declared stochastic null using:

- the same endpoint;
- the same direction;
- the same tail;
- the same inferential rule.

## Important restriction

Candidate datasets must not be selected because their observed alpha
supports the hypothesis.

The repository must not:

- evaluate all candidate outcomes;
- retain only favorable domains;
- exclude unfavorable domains after endpoint observation;
- alter preprocessing to obtain eligibility;
- change the replication threshold after observing results.

---

## Confirmation reservation

A candidate dataset used to optimize:

- the frequency band;
- the endpoint;
- the null;
- the nuisance treatment;
- the replication threshold;

must not subsequently be represented as untouched confirmatory
replication data.

---
