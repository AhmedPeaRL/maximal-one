# Independent Replication Pool Rule

## Scientific role

This protocol defines how independent real-domain replication datasets
must be selected for future confirmatory evaluation.

Replication eligibility is determined before observing the confirmatory
endpoint.

---

## Minimum requirement

The scientific claim requires:

- one primary real domain;
- at least two independent secondary real domains.

---

## Independence requirement

A secondary domain must differ from the primary domain in its real-world
measurement process or scientific domain.

Derived transformations of the primary dataset do not count.

Synthetic data do not count.

Null-generated data do not count.

Shuffle controls do not count.

---

## Eligibility before outcome inspection

The following must be established before endpoint evaluation:

- dataset identity;
- scientific domain;
- sampling frequency;
- minimum length;
- preprocessing rule;
- missing-data rule;
- canonical estimator compatibility;
- canonical frequency-band compatibility;
- null-family compatibility.

A dataset must not become eligible or ineligible because its observed
result is favorable or unfavorable.

---

## Endpoint requirement

The same declared endpoint must be used.

The same direction must be used.

The same inferential tail must be used.

The same declared null family must be used.

Domain-level null rejection is required.

---

## Short-series rule

A dataset failing the declared replication-length requirement is not
replication evidence.

It must remain reported as ineligible.

It must not be padded, interpolated, duplicated, or otherwise altered
to satisfy the threshold.

---

## Fresh confirmation rule

Future confirmatory replication datasets must not have been used to
optimize:

- the frequency band;
- the endpoint;
- the null family;
- the nuisance treatment;
- the replication threshold.

---

## Claim gate

If fewer than two eligible independent secondary domains demonstrate the
declared domain-level result, claim promotion remains blocked.

---

## HCM boundary

Replication status makes no inference about HCM causation, consciousness,
NEF, new physics, universality, or mechanism.
