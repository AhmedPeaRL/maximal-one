# Solar Periodicity Nuisance Rule

## Status

This document defines a prospective methodological decision rule.

It is not a claim-supporting result and does not modify any historical
confirmatory interpretation.

---

## Purpose

The primary real dataset contains temporal structure associated with
solar activity. A major approximately decadal component may occur near
the low-frequency boundary of the canonical spectral analysis.

The null protocol must therefore explicitly determine whether known
periodic structure is treated as nuisance structure before any fresh
confirmatory analysis.

---

## Prohibited procedure

The project MUST NOT decide whether the solar-cycle component is nuisance
structure by selecting the interpretation that produces a favorable
scientific p-value.

The endpoint p-value MUST NOT be used to choose the nuisance treatment.

---

## Required assessment

Before fresh confirmation, the protocol must characterize:

1. the frequency of the dominant known periodic component;
2. its harmonics;
3. spectral leakage into the canonical `0.01-0.05 band`;
4. the effect of the declared detrending procedure;
5. the effect of the declared temporal aggregation;
6. whether the proposed short-memory null preserves or inadequately
   represents this nuisance structure.

---

## Decision principle

If a known deterministic or quasi-deterministic periodic component
contributes materially to the declared endpoint band, its treatment must
be specified as part of the null or preprocessing protocol before the
confirmation dataset is analyzed.

If it does not materially contribute to the endpoint band under the
declared estimator and finite-sample procedure, the protocol may retain
the existing treatment, provided that the decision rule and diagnostic
evidence are archived.

---

## Confirmatory prohibition

No nuisance-treatment decision made after observing the confirmatory
endpoint may be represented as preregistered evidence.

---

## Scientific interpretation

This assessment does not establish or falsify the Constrained Spectral
Persistence Hypothesis.

It exists solely to ensure that the stochastic null represents the
declared nuisance structure without absorbing the hypothesized effect
through post-observation model selection.

---

## HCM boundary

This protocol makes no inference about consciousness, NEF, HCM
causation, new physics, universality, or mechanism.
