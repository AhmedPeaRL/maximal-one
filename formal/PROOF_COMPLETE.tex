\section{Proof Strategy}

We proceed in three steps:

\subsection{Structural Decomposition}
Decompose $p_n$ into irreducible components 
under the Archimedean constraint.

\subsection{Moment Matrix Rank Growth}
Analyze the growth of the associated moment matrix rank 
as a function of $n$.

\subsection{Degree Separation}
Show that any SOS representation requires degree 
at least proportional to the spectral growth of $p_n$.

\subsection{Conclusion}
Combining structural and spectral growth yields the desired bound.
