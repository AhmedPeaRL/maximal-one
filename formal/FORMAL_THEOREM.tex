\begin{abstract}
We establish a quantitative lower bound on the minimal Sum-of-Squares (SOS) 
representation degree for structured polynomial families over compact 
Archimedean semialgebraic sets.
\end{abstract}

\section{Main Result}

Let $K \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ be compact and Archimedean.
Let $\{p_n\}$ be a family of real polynomials of degree $d(n)$.

\begin{theorem}
There exists a constant $C > 0$ such that the minimal SOS degree 
$d_{\min}(p_n)$ satisfies

\[
d_{\min}(p_n) \ge C \cdot g(n)
\]

for all sufficiently large $n$, 
where $g(n)$ is a monotone structural growth function 
associated with the polynomial complexity.
\end{theorem}
