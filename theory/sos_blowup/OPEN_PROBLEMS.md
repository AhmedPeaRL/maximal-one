# OPEN PROBLEMS

1. Explicit SOS Degree Bounds

Determine effective upper bounds for degrees in Putinar representations
under curvature and non-degeneracy conditions.

---

2. Boundary Vanishing Order vs SOS Complexity

Investigate how the vanishing order of f at boundary points
affects minimal SOS degree.

---

3. Uniform Blow-up Constants

Under positive definite Hessian assumptions,
derive explicit lower bounds:

    f(x) ≥ c · dist(x, Z)^2

with computable c.

---

4. Functional Duality Extension

Study moment functional representations under weakened Archimedean conditions.

---

5. Non-Archimedean Extensions

Extend analysis to ordered fields beyond ℝ.

---

These problems are open and no solution is claimed here.
