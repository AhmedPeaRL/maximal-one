# Theorem Statement (Rigorously Bounded Form)

## Ambient Setting

Let K = ℝ.
Let R = ℝ[x₁, …, xₙ] be the real polynomial ring in n variables.
Let Σ ⊂ R denote the cone of sums of squares (SOS) polynomials.

Let M ⊂ R be a quadratic module generated by polynomials g₁, …, g_m:

    M = { σ₀ + ∑_{i=1}^m σ_i g_i  |  σ_i ∈ Σ }

We assume:

(A1) M is Archimedean:
There exists N > 0 such that N - ∑ x_i² ∈ M.

(A2) The feasible set
    S = { x ∈ ℝⁿ | g_i(x) ≥ 0 }
is compact (implied by A1).

---

## Proven Statement (Conditional on Classical Results)

Let f ∈ R satisfy:

    f(x) > 0   for all x ∈ S.

Then, by Putinar’s Positivstellensatz:

    f ∈ M.

That is, f admits a representation:

    f = σ₀ + ∑ σ_i g_i

with σ_i ∈ Σ.

This statement is fully classical and relies only on the Archimedean property.

---

## Blow-up Context (Local Formulation)

Let x₀ ∈ S be a boundary point where f vanishes:

    f(x₀) = 0.

Assume:

(B1) f ≥ 0 on S.
(B2) ∇f(x₀) = 0.
(B3) The Hessian D²f(x₀) is positive definite.

Then locally near x₀:

    f(x) = Q₂(x - x₀) + o(‖x - x₀‖²)

where Q₂ is a positive definite quadratic form.

---

## What Is Proven

• Local quadratic expansion under smoothness assumptions.
• Strict positivity ⇒ SOS representation (via Putinar).
• Compactness from Archimedean assumption.

---

## What Is NOT Proven Here

• Degree bounds on the SOS representation.
• Uniform asymptotic blow-up rates globally.
• Automatic positivity without assumptions.
• Any non-classical extension beyond known Positivstellensatz results.

---

## Conjectural Extension (Clearly Marked)

Conjecture:
Under suitable non-degeneracy and curvature growth conditions,
a uniform SOS degree bound may exist that scales polynomially
with the vanishing order of f at boundary points.

This remains open and is not claimed as proven.
