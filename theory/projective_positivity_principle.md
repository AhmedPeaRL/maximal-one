# Projective Positivity Principle (Restricted Form)

Let p(x) be a coercive polynomial on ℝⁿ.

Define the homogeneous lift:

    p̃(x0, x) = x0^d p(x / x0)

where d = deg(p).

Then positivity of p on ℝⁿ
is equivalent to positivity of p̃
on the compact subset of the projective sphere
defined by x0 ≥ 0 and ||(x0, x)|| = 1.

This equivalence holds provided:

1. The leading homogeneous form of p is positive definite.
2. p is coercive.

This induces compactification of ℝⁿ
via projective embedding.
