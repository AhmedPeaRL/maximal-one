# Lorenz-96 Status

## Current scientific status

The Lorenz-96 experiments are retained as historical computational
experiments.

They are NOT part of the current machine-gated scientific claim:

Constrained Spectral Persistence Hypothesis.

---

## Evidence classification

Lorenz-96 results are classified as:

`historical / synthetic / non-primary evidence`

They do not establish:

- the current primary spectral hypothesis;
- independent real-domain replication;
- stochastic-null adequacy;
- HCM causation;
- consciousness;
- universality;
- mechanism;
- new physics.

---

## Preservation principle

Historical results are preserved for provenance.

They are not deleted merely because the current scientific question
has changed.

They are also not promoted merely because historical metrics were
stronger.

---

## Current role

Lorenz-96 may be revisited later as an independent benchmark for a
separate hypothesis.

Any such reuse requires a separately declared protocol.

---

## Epistemic boundary

Historical Lorenz-96 results MUST NOT be combined with current
sunspot-domain evidence to manufacture a stronger confirmatory result.
